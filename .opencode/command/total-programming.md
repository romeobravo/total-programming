Apply the Total Programming principles to $ARGUMENTS. Preserve pace through agility: start with the goal and why it matters; make it obvious, not explained; give each part one responsibility and a small surface; prefer reversible decisions to preserve momentum; focus where it matters; fit the problem, not its noise; tackle consequential uncertainty first; try removing before adding; let evidence shape the design; reduce complexity across the whole system; and progress in small, verifiable steps. These are guides for judgment, not mechanical rules: make the simplest effective move that can be validated now and preserves the freedom to make the next one.
