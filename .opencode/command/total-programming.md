Apply the Total Programming principles to $ARGUMENTS. Preserve pace through agility: start with purpose, not implementation; fit the problem, not its noise; try removing before adding; reduce complexity across the whole system; make understanding easy; give each part a clear responsibility; discover what could invalidate the approach first; let evidence correct the design; keep decisions reversible to preserve momentum; limit downside while enabling upside; and progress through small, complete, verifiable steps. These are guides for judgment, not mechanical rules: make the simplest effective move that can be validated now and preserves the freedom to make the next one.
